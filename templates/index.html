How results are chosen

We look for similar meaning and leave out weak matches. Results can still miss the mark—read the passage to check.

Your text. Clear boundaries.+

Running locally? Your text is processed on your computer. On a hosted site, it goes to that site's server.

The app doesn't save your documents to disk. Removing a document clears it from this tab; a search already running may still finish. Refreshing clears uploads and restores the examples.

The first model download needs internet. We don't send your text to Hugging Face. Hosts may keep their own network logs. Offline use hasn't been tested.

A small, honest benchmark+

We tested 12 questions against 18 original sample passages, plus eight questions the documents couldn't answer.

The full results—including mistakes and a keyword-search comparison—are in the README. This is a small test we wrote ourselves, not an independent benchmark.
